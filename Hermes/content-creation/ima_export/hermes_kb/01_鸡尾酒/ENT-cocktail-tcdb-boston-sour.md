# Boston Sour

**Boston Sour**

## 概述

Boston Sour是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于威士忌酸酒杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 13.0% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：威士忌酸酒杯（Whiskey sour glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Blended whiskey | 2 oz |
| Lemon | Juice of 1/2 |
| Powdered sugar | 1 tsp |
| Egg white | 1 |
| Lemon | 1 slice |
| Cherry | 1 |

## 调制方法

Shake juice of lemon, powdered sugar, blended whiskey, and egg white with cracked ice and strain into a whiskey sour glass. Add the slice of lemon, top with the cherry, and serve.
