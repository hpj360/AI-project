# Hunter's Moon

**Hunter's Moon**

## 概述

Hunter's Moon是一款鸡尾酒，使用5种配料调制，盛于Balloon Glass。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 18% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Cocktail
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：Balloon Glass（Balloon Glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Vermouth | 25 ml |
| Maraschino Cherry | 15 ml |
| Sugar Syrup | 10 ml |
| Lemonade | 100 ml |
| Blackberries | 2 |

## 调制方法

Put the Bombay Sapphire, Martini Bianco, sugar syrup & blackberries in a cocktail shaker with lots of ice and shake vigorously before pouring into a balloon glass, topping up with lemonade and garnishing with a wedge of orange.
