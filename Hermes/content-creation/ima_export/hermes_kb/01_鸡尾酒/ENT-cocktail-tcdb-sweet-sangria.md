# Sweet Sangria

**Sweet Sangria**

## 概述

Sweet Sangria是一款派对宾治，使用8种配料调制，盛于Pitcher。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 18% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Punch / Party Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：Pitcher（Pitcher）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Red wine | 2 bottles |
| Sugar | 1 cup |
| Water | 2 cups hot |
| Apple | 1 cup |
| Orange | wedges |
| Lime | wedges |
| Lemon | 适量 |
| Fresca | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Dissolve the sugar in hot water and cool. Peel the citrus fruits and break into wedges. Mix the wine, sugar syrup, fruit, and Fresca in a pitcher and put in the fridge for a few hours. Serve in tall glasses with a straw.
