# Long vodka

**Long vodka**

## 概述

Long vodka是一款经典饮品，使用5种配料调制，盛于高球杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 9.3% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：高球杯（Highball glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Vodka | 5 cl |
| Lime | 1/2 |
| Angostura bitters | 4 dashes |
| Tonic water | 1 dl Schweppes |
| Ice | 4 |

## 调制方法

Shake a tall glass with ice cubes and Angostura, coating the inside of the glass. Por the vodka onto this, add 1 slice of lime and squeeze juice out of remainder, mix with tonic, stir and voila you have a Long Vodka
