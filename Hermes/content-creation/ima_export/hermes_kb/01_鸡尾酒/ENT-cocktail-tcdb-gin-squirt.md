# Gin Squirt

**Gin Squirt**

## 概述

Gin Squirt是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于高球杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 14.1% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：高球杯（Highball glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Gin | 1 1/2 oz |
| Grenadine | 1 tsp |
| Powdered sugar | 1 tblsp |
| Pineapple | 3 chunks |
| Strawberries | 2 |
| Carbonated water | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Stir gin, grenadine, and powdered sugar with ice and strain into a highball glass over ice cubes. Fill with carbonated water and stir. Decorate with the pineapple chunks and the strawberries and serve.
