# Sangria

**Sangria**

## 概述

Sangria是一款派对宾治，使用6种配料调制，盛于Pitcher。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 18% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Punch / Party Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：Pitcher（Pitcher）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Red wine | 1 bottle |
| Sugar | 1/2 cup |
| Orange juice | 1 cup |
| Lemon juice | 1 cup |
| Cloves | 适量 |
| Cinnamon | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Mix all together in a pitcher and refrigerate. Add cloves and cinnamon sticks to taste. Serve in wine glasses.
