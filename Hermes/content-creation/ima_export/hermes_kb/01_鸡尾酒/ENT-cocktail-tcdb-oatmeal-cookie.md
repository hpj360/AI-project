# Oatmeal Cookie

**Oatmeal Cookie**

## 概述

Oatmeal Cookie是一款鸡尾酒，使用5种配料调制，盛于Mason jar。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 7.4% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Cocktail
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：Mason jar（Mason jar）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Kahlua | 2 parts |
| Baileys irish cream | 2 parts |
| Butterscotch schnapps | 4 parts |
| Jagermeister | 1 part |
| Goldschlager | 1/2 part |

## 调制方法

Just mix it all together.
It's meant to be a shot, but it works just fine as a proper adult-sized drink over lots of ice.

Tastes like an oatmeal cookie.
