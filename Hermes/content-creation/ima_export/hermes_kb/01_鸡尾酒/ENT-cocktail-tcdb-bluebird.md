# Bluebird

**Bluebird**

## 概述

Bluebird是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于鸡尾酒杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 17.3% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：鸡尾酒杯（Cocktail glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Gin | 1 1/2 oz |
| Triple sec | 1/2 oz |
| Blue Curacao | 1/2 oz |
| Bitters | 2 dashes |
| Maraschino cherry | 1 |
| Lemon peel | 1 twist of |

## 调制方法

In a mixing glass half-filled with crushed ice, combine the gin, triple sec, Curacao, and bitters. Stir well. Strain into a cocktail glass and garnish with the lemon twist and the cherry.
