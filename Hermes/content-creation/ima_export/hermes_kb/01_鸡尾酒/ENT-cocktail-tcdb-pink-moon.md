# Pink Moon

**Pink Moon**

## 概述

Pink Moon是一款鸡尾酒，使用5种配料调制，盛于Whiskey Glass。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 10.2% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Cocktail
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：Whiskey Glass（Whiskey Glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Gin | 1 shot |
| Coconut Liqueur | 1 shot |
| Elderflower cordial | 25 ml |
| Lime Juice | 30 ml |
| Blackberries | Garnish with |

## 调制方法

Slowly shake in a shaker with ice, strain into a square whiskey glass. Top with fresh ice. Add the blackberries to garnish. Add flowers and a green leaf for a special look!
