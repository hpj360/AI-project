# English Highball

**English Highball**

## 概述

English Highball是一款经典饮品，使用5种配料调制，盛于高球杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 32.7% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：高球杯（Highball glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Brandy | 3/4 oz |
| Gin | 3/4 oz |
| Sweet Vermouth | 3/4 oz |
| Carbonated water | 适量 |
| Lemon peel | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Pour brandy, gin, and sweet vermouth into a highball glass over ice cubes. Fill with carbonated water. Add the twist of lemon peel, stir, and serve. (Ginger ale may be substituted for carbonated water, if preferred.)
