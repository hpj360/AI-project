# English Rose Cocktail

**English Rose Cocktail**

## 概述

English Rose Cocktail是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于鸡尾酒杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 26.4% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：鸡尾酒杯（Cocktail glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Apricot brandy | 3/4 oz |
| Gin | 1 1/2 oz |
| Dry Vermouth | 3/4 oz |
| Grenadine | 1 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 1/4 tsp |
| Cherry | 1 |

## 调制方法

Rub rim of cocktail glass with lemon juice and dip rim of glass in powdered sugar. Shake all ingredients (except cherry) with ice and strain into sugar-rimmed glass. Top with the cherry and serve.
