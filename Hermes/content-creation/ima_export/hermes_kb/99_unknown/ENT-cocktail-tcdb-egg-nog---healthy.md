# Egg Nog - Healthy

**Egg Nog - Healthy**

## 概述

Egg Nog - Healthy是一款派对宾治，使用7种配料调制，盛于柯林斯杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 18% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Punch / Party Drink
- **含酒精**：Optional alcohol
- **推荐杯具**：柯林斯杯（Collins Glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Egg | 1/2 cup |
| Sugar | 3 tblsp |
| Condensed milk | 13 oz skimmed |
| Milk | 3/4 cup skimmed |
| Vanilla extract | 1 tsp |
| Rum | 1 tsp |
| Nutmeg | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Whip egg substitute and sugar together, combine with the two kinds of milk, vanilla, and rum. Mix well. Chill over night. Sprinkle with nutmeg. Makes 6 servings.
