# New York Sour

**New York Sour**

## 概述

New York Sour是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于威士忌酸酒杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 25.3% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：威士忌酸酒杯（Whiskey sour glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Blended whiskey | 2 oz |
| Lemon | Juice of 1/2 |
| Sugar | 1 tsp |
| Red wine | (Claret) |
| Lemon | 适量 |
| Cherry | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Shake blended whiskey, juice of lemon, and powdered sugar with ice and strain into a whiskey sour glass. Float claret on top. Decorate with the half-slice of lemon and the cherry and serve.
