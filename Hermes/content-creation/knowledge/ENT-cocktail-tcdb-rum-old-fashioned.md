# Rum Old-fashioned

**Rum Old-fashioned**

## 概述

Rum Old-fashioned是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于古典杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 23.0% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：古典杯（Old-fashioned glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Light rum | 1 1/2 oz |
| 151 proof rum | 1 tsp |
| Powdered sugar | 1/2 tsp |
| Bitters | 1 dash |
| Water | 1 tsp |
| Lime peel | Twist of |

## 调制方法

Stir powdered sugar, water, and bitters in an old-fashioned glass. When sugar has dissolved add ice cubes and light rum. Add the twist of lime peel, float 151 proof rum on top, and serve.
