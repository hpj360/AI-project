# Zipperhead

**Zipperhead**

## 概述

Zipperhead是一款烈酒shot，使用3种配料调制，盛于威士忌酸酒杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 15.0% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Shot
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：威士忌酸酒杯（Whiskey sour glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Chambord raspberry liqueur | 1 shot |
| Vodka | 1 shot |
| Soda water | Fill with |

## 调制方法

Fill glass with rocks, add straw before putting in liquor. Then add the ingredients in order, trying to keep layered as much as possible (i.e. Chambord on bottom, then Vodka, Then soda on top).
