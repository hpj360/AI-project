# Brain Fart

**Brain Fart**

## 概述

Brain Fart是一款派对宾治，使用6种配料调制，盛于宾治盆。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 13.3% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Punch / Party Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：宾治盆（Punch bowl）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Everclear | 1 fifth |
| Vodka | 1 fifth Smirnoff red label |
| Mountain Dew | 2 L |
| Surge | 2 L |
| Lemon juice | 1 small bottle |
| Rum | 1 pint |

## 调制方法

Mix all ingredients together. Slowly and gently. Works best if ice is added to punch bowl and soda's are very cold.
