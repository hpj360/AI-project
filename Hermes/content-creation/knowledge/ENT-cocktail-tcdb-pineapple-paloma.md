# Pineapple Paloma

**Pineapple Paloma**

## 概述

Pineapple Paloma是一款鸡尾酒，使用6种配料调制，盛于柯林斯杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 8.4% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Cocktail
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：柯林斯杯（Collins glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Tequila | 4 oz |
| Grapefruit Juice | 4 oz |
| Fresh Lime Juice | 1 oz |
| Pineapple Juice | 8 oz |
| Lime | Garnish with |
| Pepper | Rimmed |

## 调制方法

Rub the rim of each glass with lime slice and dip into salt.
Add ice, tequila, grapefruit juice, lime juice and top with pineapple soda.
Give it a quick stir.
Garnish with fresh pineapple or lime.
