# Lady Love Fizz

**Lady Love Fizz**

## 概述

Lady Love Fizz是一款经典饮品，使用6种配料调制，盛于鸡尾酒杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 17.8% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：鸡尾酒杯（Cocktail glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Gin | 2 oz |
| Light cream | 2 tsp |
| Powdered sugar | 1 tsp |
| Lemon | Juice of 1/2 |
| Egg white | 1 |
| Carbonated water | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Shake all ingredients (except carbonated water) with ice and strain into a cocktail glass over two ice cubes. Fill with carbonated water, stir, and serve.
