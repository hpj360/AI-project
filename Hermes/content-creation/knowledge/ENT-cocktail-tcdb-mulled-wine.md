# Mulled Wine

**Mulled Wine**

## 概述

Mulled Wine是一款派对宾治，使用7种配料调制，盛于柯林斯杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 15.2% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Punch / Party Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：柯林斯杯（Collins Glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Water | 3 cups |
| Sugar | 1 cup |
| Cloves | 12 |
| Cinnamon | 2 |
| Lemon peel | 1 |
| Red wine | 750 ml |
| Brandy | 1/4 cup |

## 调制方法

Simmer 3 cups water with, sugar, cloves, cinnamon sticks, and lemon peel in a stainless steel pot for 10 minutes. Add wine heat to a "coffee temperature" (DO NOT BOIL) then add the brandy.
