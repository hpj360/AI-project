# Sol Y Sombra

**Sol Y Sombra**

## 概述

Sol Y Sombra是一款经典饮品，使用2种配料调制，盛于Brandy snifter。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 20.0% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Ordinary Drink
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：Brandy snifter（Brandy snifter）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Brandy | 1 1/2 oz |
| Anisette | 1 1/2 oz |

## 调制方法

Shake ingredients with ice, strain into a brandy snifter, and serve. (The English translation of the name of this drink is "Sun and Shade", and after sampling this drink, you'll understand why. Thanks, Kirby.)
