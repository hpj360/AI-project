# Jamaican Coffee

**Jamaican Coffee**

## 概述

Jamaican Coffee是一款咖啡/茶饮，使用4种配料调制，盛于香槟杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 13.3% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Coffee / Tea
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：香槟杯（Champagne flute）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Rum | 1/6 glass |
| Coffee | 1/6 glass strong black |
| Water | 1/2 glass cold |
| Whipped cream | 适量 |

## 调制方法

Stir the rum, coffee and water together. Top with the whipped cream. Sprinkle with a pinch of well ground coffee and drink with a straw.
