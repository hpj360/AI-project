# Lunch Box

**Lunch Box**

## 概述

Lunch Box是一款啤酒鸡尾酒，使用3种配料调制，盛于品脱杯。

## 基本信息

- **酒精度**：约 13.4% ABV（估算）
- **分类**：Beer
- **含酒精**：Alcoholic
- **推荐杯具**：品脱杯（Pint glass）

## 配方

| 配料 | 用量 |
|------|------|
| Beer | 3/4 bottle |
| Amaretto | 1 shot |
| Orange juice | 1 oz |

## 调制方法

Fill a pint glass almost full with beer. Then fill the rest with orange juice (careful not to fill it to the top). Then take the shot of Amaretto and drop it in.
